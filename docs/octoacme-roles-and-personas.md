# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owners / Business Sponsors

### Role Summary
Product Owners and Business Sponsors connect project delivery to business outcomes. They clarify the desired value, make or authorize scope and priority trade-offs, and ensure the delivered solution addresses stakeholder needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate business outcomes and success measures
- Prioritize scope and clarify requirements with Product Managers
- Approve material scope, priority, and acceptance trade-offs
- Secure business decisions and support when issues require escalation
- Confirm that delivered outcomes meet business and customer needs

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to translate product strategy into project priorities
- Provide Project Managers with timely decisions for scope, schedule, and risk trade-offs
- Collaborate with Developers on acceptance criteria without directing implementation details
- Engage stakeholders and executives when decisions exceed the project team's authority

---

## Delivery Leads / Project Leads

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads or Project Leads coordinate day-to-day execution across teams. They turn the project plan into actionable work, maintain delivery momentum, and surface dependencies or blockers early.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate execution against milestones, plans, and commitments
- Track cross-team dependencies, blockers, and delivery risks
- Facilitate delivery ceremonies and maintain action items
- Keep plans, delivery forecasts, and decision records current
- Escalate deviations and recommend corrective actions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Project Managers to maintain the overall plan, status reporting, and escalation process
- Coordinate Developers and technical partners without replacing their technical ownership
- Align with Product Managers and Product Owners on priorities and readiness
- Provide stakeholders with factual progress updates and early warning of delivery concerns

---

## Technical Advisors / Architecture Partners

### Role Summary
Technical Advisors or Architecture Partners provide technical direction and feasibility guidance. They help the team make sound design decisions while leaving implementation ownership with Developers and engineering leads.

### Responsibilities
- Assess solution feasibility, technical dependencies, and architectural risks
- Define or review technical approaches and non-functional requirements
- Identify integration, security, scalability, and operational concerns
- Advise on sequencing work to reduce technical risk
- Support technical decision records and exception reviews

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Developers and engineering leads during design and implementation reviews
- Advise Project Managers and Delivery Leads on technical risk, estimates, and dependencies
- Partner with Product Managers and Product Owners to explain technical trade-offs in terms of customer and business impact
- Escalate significant architecture or risk decisions to the appropriate governance or executive stakeholders

---

## Quality and Risk Partners

### Role Summary
Quality and Risk Partners provide focused oversight of quality, governance, compliance, and delivery risk. They help teams identify issues early and establish evidence that outcomes meet agreed standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define or review quality measures, test expectations, and readiness criteria
- Monitor risks, issues, assumptions, and mitigations throughout delivery
- Verify that governance, compliance, and documentation needs are addressed
- Facilitate quality or risk reviews at key project checkpoints
- Escalate unacceptable exposure and track remediation to closure

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Project Managers to maintain risk registers, issue logs, and escalation paths
- Collaborate with Developers on test strategy, defect trends, and release readiness
- Align with Product Managers and Product Owners on acceptance and outcome measures
- Provide independent visibility to sponsors, executives, and stakeholders when risks threaten project objectives

---

## Stakeholder Managers / Communications Leads

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Managers or Communications Leads create a consistent flow of information between the project team, decision-makers, and affected groups. They support engagement and make sure the right audiences understand decisions, progress, and impacts.

### Responsibilities
- Identify stakeholder groups, communication needs, and engagement risks
- Define communication cadence, channels, and message ownership
- Prepare clear status updates, decision summaries, and change-impact communications
- Gather stakeholder feedback and route it to the accountable decision-maker
- Identify misalignment early and coordinate resolution or escalation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Project Managers on status reporting, meeting plans, and stakeholder registers
- Coordinate with Product Managers and Product Owners to communicate product direction and priority changes
- Work with Developers and Technical Advisors to translate technical impacts for non-technical audiences
- Support sponsors and executives with concise decision briefs and escalation context

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- When a person holds more than one role, make the responsibilities and decision rights explicit for the scenario.
- Use the interaction descriptions to clarify handoffs, collaboration, and escalation paths.
